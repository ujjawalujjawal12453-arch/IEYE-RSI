👁 IEYE RIS — DIKKAT AUR HAL
============================================

Ye madad bina internet ke bhi milti hai.

❓ 1. App khulti hai phir band ho jati hai
   Settings me jaakar app ka data clear karo, phir dobara
   setup karo. Ye Android ke keystore ki dikkat hoti hai.

❓ 2. Awaaz nahi sun raha
   1. Settings -> Apps -> IEYE RIS -> Permissions -> Mic ON
   2. Play Store se 'Google' app update karo
   3. Beech wala IRIS daba kar bolo

❓ 3. Bolne ke baad kuch nahi hota
   Internet check karo. AI ke liye net chahiye. Sirf torch,
   volume, app kholna — ye bina net ke chalte hain.

❓ 4. Poori baat khatam hone se pehle hi kaam shuru
   Settings -> Nakhre -> 'Sabr' ya 'Bahut sabr' chuno.
   Tab wo zyada der intezaar karega.

❓ 5. Screen ke andar ka kaam nahi karta
   Accessibility ON karni padegi:
   Settings -> Accessibility -> IEYE RIS -> ON
   Iske bina sirf app khol sakta hai, andar kuch nahi.

❓ 6. Bubble nahi dikh raha
   Settings -> Apps -> IEYE RIS -> 'Display over other apps' ON

❓ 7. Reminder nahi bajta
   Android 12+ me: Settings -> Apps -> IEYE RIS ->
   'Alarms & reminders' ON karo.

❓ 8. App khud band ho jati hai
   Settings -> Battery -> IEYE RIS -> 'Unrestricted' karo.
   Warna Android background me maar deta hai.

❓ 9. Wake word kaam nahi karta
   👂 wala switch ON karo. Aur dhyan rakho — battery zyada
   khayega kyunki mic hamesha sunta rehta hai.

❓ 10. Sab ruk gaya / kuch galat ho raha hai
   Neeche laal 🛑 IEYE RIS STOP daba do. Sab turant ruk
   jayega — agent, awaaz, mic.
